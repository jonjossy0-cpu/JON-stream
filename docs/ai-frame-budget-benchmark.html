How to read the result

At 30 FPS, a frame budget is about 33.3 ms; at 60 FPS it is about 16.7 ms. A CPU synthetic task finishing under this budget does not mean a super-resolution model can meet it: model inference, memory transfer, video capture, GPU contention, heat and sustained performance all add cost.

Do not enable AI from this score alone. Compare against real playback baseline and test for sustained dropped frames, buffering, heat, artifacts, and A/V sync.
